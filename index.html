How the launch works

Bankr + Doppler, plain language.

Launched on Bankr. Token and first liquidity went live in one transaction. The pool is JENSEN against NVDAc on Uniswap v4.

  • 100,000,000,000 JENSEN minted at deploy, 11 Sep 2026 — nothing minted before or since (verify: token contract).
  • 85,000,000,000 JENSEN (85%) was routed into the Uniswap v4 pool as initial liquidity against NVDAc, in the same transaction as the token deploy — atomic, not a separate step.
  • 15,000,000,000 JENSEN (15%) was allocated under an onchain vesting schedule — 30-day cliff, then vesting over 365 days — to 0xc59b0c07…25023 ↗. This is the creator/deployer allocation, not a fee-recipient claim we're making up.
  • Pool type: Uniswap v4 via Bankr's scheduled multicurve flow (contract is DopplerERC20V1, verified on Basescan).
  • No liquidity-migration transaction has occurred since deploy — check the token's transaction history yourself rather than taking our word that it never will.

We're not publishing a swap-fee percentage or fee-recipient address here — the launch tx shows a decaying anti-snipe fee in the first seconds after deploy, not a simple flat rate, and we haven't independently confirmed a steady-state fee split. FAQ

Ask before you ape.

Is this NVIDIA or Jensen Huang?

No. Community token. No equity, no dividends, no official endorsement.

Does $JENSEN give me NVDA?

No. NVDAc is the tokenized-stock certificate. JENSEN is a separate ERC-20 that trades against NVDAc.

Can liquidity be pulled?

This launch's LP sits in the Uniswap v4 pool with no migration transaction so far — check the token's transaction history yourself. That is not the same as "price cannot go to zero."

Why 100 billion supply?

Bankr's default. Price per token looks tiny; market cap is the number that matters.

Taxes / honeypot?

Treat it as a standard Bankr/Doppler ERC-20 unless a scanner shows otherwise. We won't claim "0/0 tax audited" without having verified the implementation ourselves.

What should I watch?

Pool depth in NVDAc, not only USD. Afterbook basis when cash NVDA is closed. Buy/sell count. Holder count. The contract address.
